The Luminos AI dashboard is your central hub for configuring AI features, monitoring performance, and managing your account. This article gives you a quick tour of the main areas.

## Dashboard layout

When you log in, you land on the **Overview** dashboard. It shows key metrics at a glance:

- **Revenue influenced** — total revenue attributed to AI-powered features in the selected time period
- **Recommendation clicks** — click-through rate on product recommendation widgets
- **Search conversion** — percentage of searches that lead to a purchase
- **Chatbot resolution rate** — percentage of chat conversations resolved without human handoff

Use the date picker in the top-right corner to adjust the reporting window. The default view shows the last 7 days.

## Navigation

The left sidebar organises the platform into modules:

- **Recommendations** — configure models, placements, personalisation rules, and A/B tests
- **Search** — manage Smart Search settings, synonyms, merchandising rules, and analytics
- **Inventory** — view forecasts, set alerts, and configure reorder automation
- **Pricing** — create dynamic pricing rules, track competitors, and set margin protections
- **Segmentation** — build audience segments, configure triggers, and sync to destinations
- **Chatbot** — design conversation flows, train on your knowledge base, and review analytics
- **Analytics** — access dashboards, custom reports, funnels, cohorts, and exports
- **Settings** — manage integrations, tracking, team members, billing, and security

## Workspaces

Each Luminos AI account contains one or more workspaces. A workspace maps to a single storefront or brand. If you operate multiple stores, create a separate workspace for each one under **Settings → Workspaces**.

Data is isolated between workspaces — recommendations trained in one workspace do not affect another.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press `?` anywhere in the dashboard to see available shortcuts. Common ones include:

- `⌘K` — open the command palette
- `⌘/` — toggle the left sidebar
- `G` then `R` — go to Recommendations
- `G` then `A` — go to Analytics
