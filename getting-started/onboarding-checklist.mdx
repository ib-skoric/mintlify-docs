Use this checklist to verify that your Luminos AI setup is complete before enabling AI features on your live storefront. Each item links to the relevant guide if you need to revisit a step.

## Account and data

- [ ] Account created and email verified
- [ ] [Store connected](/getting-started/connect-your-store) with products, orders, and customers syncing
- [ ] [Tracking pixel installed](/getting-started/install-tracking-pixel) and sending events
- [ ] [Product catalog](/getting-started/import-product-catalog) imported with no critical health issues
- [ ] At least 7 days of behavioural data collected (recommended for personalisation accuracy)

## AI features

- [ ] [Recommendation engine](/recommendations/setup-recommendation-engine) configured with at least one model and placement
- [ ] [Smart Search](/search/configure-smart-search) enabled with synonyms and basic merchandising rules
- [ ] [Chatbot](/chatbot/deploy-storefront-chatbot) deployed on at least the support or product pages
- [ ] [Inventory forecasting](/inventory/forecasting-models) enabled if you manage your own fulfilment
- [ ] [Dynamic pricing](/pricing/dynamic-pricing-rules) rules created with [margin protection](/pricing/margin-protection) guardrails

## Team and security

- [ ] [Team members invited](/account/team-management) with appropriate [roles and permissions](/account/roles-permissions)
- [ ] [SSO configured](/account/sso-setup) if required by your organisation
- [ ] [Audit logging](/account/audit-logs) reviewed to confirm admin actions are captured

## Compliance

- [ ] [GDPR settings](/account/gdpr-compliance) configured for your region
- [ ] [Data retention](/account/data-retention) policies reviewed and adjusted
- [ ] Privacy policy updated to mention Luminos AI data collection

## Go-live

Once every item is checked, switch your recommendation and search placements from **Draft** to **Live** in each module's settings. Monitor the [real-time metrics](/analytics/real-time-metrics) dashboard for the first hour to confirm events and AI responses are flowing correctly.

<Tip>
Schedule your go-live during a low-traffic period so you can catch issues before peak hours.
</Tip>
