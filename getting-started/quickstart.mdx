This guide takes you from creating an account to seeing AI-powered product recommendations on a test page. Follow each step in order — you can always revisit individual sections later for deeper configuration.

<Note>
You need admin access to your ecommerce platform to install the tracking pixel and connect your store.
</Note>

<Steps>

<Step title="Create your account">
Sign up at **app.luminos.ai** with your work email. Confirm the verification link, then choose a workspace name your team will recognise in notifications and reports.
</Step>

<Step title="Connect your store">
Navigate to **Settings → Integrations** and select your ecommerce platform. Luminos AI supports Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, and Magento out of the box. Authorise the connection and wait for the initial data sync — this usually takes two to five minutes for catalogs under 50,000 SKUs.

See [Connect your ecommerce store](/getting-started/connect-your-store) for platform-specific instructions.
</Step>

<Step title="Install the tracking pixel">
Go to **Settings → Tracking** and copy the JavaScript snippet. Paste it into your storefront's `<head>` tag or use your tag manager. The pixel starts recording page views, product impressions, and add-to-cart events immediately.

See [Install the tracking pixel](/getting-started/install-tracking-pixel) for tag manager instructions.
</Step>

<Step title="Enable recommendations">
Open **Recommendations → Setup** and select the **Quick Start** model. This pre-trained model uses collaborative filtering and works well even with limited historical data. Choose a placement — homepage carousel is the fastest to validate — and publish.
</Step>

<Step title="Preview and verify">
Visit your storefront in a private browser window. You should see the recommendation carousel within a few seconds. Open the Luminos AI dashboard and confirm that impression and click events appear in **Analytics → Real-time**.
</Step>

</Steps>
