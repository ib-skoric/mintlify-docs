Luminos AI brings machine learning to every part of your online store — from the products you recommend to the prices you set. This section walks you through account creation, store connection, and the first configurations that get real data flowing into the platform.

Most teams are live within a single afternoon. The quickstart guide covers the critical path, while the remaining articles in this section go deeper on each step for teams that need tighter control over data imports, tracking, and permissions.

## Where to start

If you are setting up Luminos AI for the first time, follow the guides in order:

1. [Quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart) — end-to-end setup in 30 minutes
2. [Connect your store](/getting-started/connect-your-store) — link Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, or Magento
3. [Install the tracking pixel](/getting-started/install-tracking-pixel) — capture visitor behaviour on your storefront
4. [Import your product catalog](/getting-started/import-product-catalog) — sync products, variants, and metadata
5. [Platform overview](/getting-started/platform-overview) — understand the dashboard and navigation
6. [Onboarding checklist](/getting-started/onboarding-checklist) — verify every module before go-live
