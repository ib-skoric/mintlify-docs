Lifecycle stages provide a simplified view of where each customer sits in their relationship with your brand. Unlike [RFM analysis](/segmentation/rfm-analysis) which uses three numerical dimensions, lifecycle stages assign a single, human-readable label that everyone on your team can understand.

## Default stages

Luminos AI defines six lifecycle stages based on purchase behaviour and engagement signals:

### Prospect

A visitor who has browsed your site but has not yet made a purchase. Prospects are identified by the [tracking pixel](/getting-started/install-tracking-pixel) and matched to profiles when they provide an email or create an account.

### First-time buyer

A customer who has placed exactly one order. The transition from prospect to first-time buyer is the most critical conversion in the customer lifecycle.

### Active

A customer who has placed 2 or more orders within the last 90 days. Active customers are in a healthy purchasing rhythm.

### Loyal

A customer who has placed 5 or more orders and has a recency score of 4 or higher. Loyal customers are your most predictable revenue source.

### At-risk

A customer who was previously Active or Loyal but has not purchased in the last 60–90 days (configurable). At-risk customers need re-engagement before they churn.

### Lapsed

A customer who has not purchased in over 180 days. Lapsed customers require significant incentive to return.

## Customising stages

Modify the default stage definitions under **Segmentation → Lifecycle → Settings**:

- Adjust the order count and recency thresholds for each stage
- Add custom stages (e.g. "VIP" for customers who spend above a certain threshold)
- Rename stages to match your team's terminology

## Lifecycle dashboard

The **Segmentation → Lifecycle → Dashboard** shows:

- **Stage distribution** — how many customers are in each stage (pie chart and absolute numbers)
- **Migration flow** — how customers move between stages over time (Sankey diagram)
- **Stage conversion rates** — what percentage of prospects become first-time buyers, first-time buyers become active, etc.
- **Revenue by stage** — how much revenue each stage contributes

## Using lifecycle stages

Lifecycle stages integrate with other Luminos AI features:

- **Recommendations** — show different recommendation models to prospects (trending) vs. loyal customers (personalised)
- **Pricing** — offer first-time buyer discounts or loyalty pricing
- **Chatbot** — customise chatbot greetings based on lifecycle stage
- **Triggers** — fire [behavioural triggers](/segmentation/behavioural-triggers) when customers move between stages

<Tip>
Focus your retention investment on the "At-risk" stage. Preventing a loyal customer from lapsing is far more cost-effective than acquiring a new prospect.
</Tip>
