Luminos AI's segmentation module groups your customers into meaningful audiences based on their behaviour, purchase history, demographics, and predicted future actions. Segments power targeted recommendations, pricing rules, email campaigns, and ad targeting across the platform.

Unlike static lists, Luminos AI segments update in real time — as a customer's behaviour changes, their segment membership updates automatically.

## Key capabilities

- **Behavioural segmentation** — group by browse history, purchase patterns, and engagement
- **Value-based segmentation** — RFM analysis and lifetime value scoring
- **Predictive segmentation** — AI-driven scores for purchase likelihood, churn risk, and campaign response
- **Real-time membership** — segments update as events arrive, not on a batch schedule
- **Destination sync** — push segments to email platforms, ad networks, and CRMs
