Predictive scoring uses machine learning to assign a probability score to each customer for specific future outcomes. Luminos AI provides three pre-built predictive scores and supports custom scoring models.

## Pre-built scores

### Purchase propensity

The likelihood that a customer will make a purchase in the next 7 days. Scores range from 0 to 100. Customers with a score above 70 are considered high-intent and respond well to timely nudges like personalised recommendations or cart reminders.

### Churn risk

The likelihood that an active customer will not purchase again within their expected purchase cycle. High churn risk scores (above 60) indicate customers who need re-engagement. This score considers purchase recency, frequency trends, and engagement decay.

### Campaign response

The likelihood that a customer will engage with a marketing campaign (open, click, or convert). Use this score to focus campaign spend on customers most likely to respond, improving ROI and reducing unsubscribes.

## How scores are calculated

Each predictive model trains on your historical customer data:

- **Features** — purchase history, browsing behaviour, email engagement, recommendation interactions, time-based patterns
- **Training data** — the model learns from customers who did and did not exhibit the target behaviour in the past
- **Validation** — accuracy is measured using a held-out test set and reported as AUC (area under the ROC curve)

Models retrain weekly to incorporate the latest data.

## Using predictive scores

Predictive scores can be used as segment filters. Go to **Segmentation → Segments → Create** and add a predictive score condition:

- **Purchase propensity > 70** — create a "High intent" segment for personalised homepage recommendations
- **Churn risk > 60** — create an "At-risk" segment for re-engagement triggers
- **Campaign response > 50** — create a "Responsive" segment for your next email campaign

## Custom scoring models

If you need a prediction that the pre-built scores do not cover (e.g. likelihood to refer a friend, likelihood to upgrade to a higher plan), create a custom model:

1. Go to **Segmentation → Predictive → Custom → Create**
2. Define the target event (the behaviour you want to predict)
3. Select the training window (how far back to look for examples)
4. Luminos AI trains the model and reports accuracy metrics
5. If accuracy is acceptable (AUC > 0.65), activate the model

<Tip>
Predictive scores are most useful when combined with other segment filters. "High churn risk" alone is too broad — "High churn risk AND loyal lifecycle stage" identifies the customers worth saving.
</Tip>

## Accuracy monitoring

Model accuracy can degrade over time as customer behaviour evolves. Monitor accuracy under **Segmentation → Predictive → Model health**. Luminos AI alerts you when a model's AUC drops below 0.60 and suggests retraining.
