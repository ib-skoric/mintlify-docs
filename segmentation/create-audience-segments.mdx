Audience segments group customers who share common characteristics. Use them to personalise recommendations, target pricing rules, or sync audiences to marketing platforms.

## Segment builder

<Steps>

<Step title="Start a new segment">
Go to **Segmentation → Segments → Create**. Give the segment a descriptive name (e.g. "High-value repeat buyers" or "Cart abandoners last 7 days").
</Step>

<Step title="Add filter conditions">
Build your segment using filters. Combine multiple conditions with AND/OR logic:

**Purchase behaviour:**
- Total orders (e.g. > 3 orders)
- Total spend (e.g. > £500 lifetime)
- Last purchase date (e.g. within the last 30 days)
- Average order value (e.g. > £75)
- Product categories purchased

**Browsing behaviour:**
- Pages viewed in the last N days
- Products viewed but not purchased
- Search queries used
- Time on site

**Demographics:**
- Location (country, region, city)
- Account age
- Email domain (useful for B2B segmentation)

**Engagement:**
- Email open rate
- Chatbot interaction
- Recommendation clicks
</Step>

<Step title="Preview the segment">
Click **Preview** to see how many customers match the current criteria and a sample of matching profiles. Adjust filters until the segment size and composition look right.
</Step>

<Step title="Save and activate">
Save the segment. It becomes available immediately for use in recommendations, pricing rules, and destination syncing. Membership updates in real time as new events arrive.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Segment types

### Dynamic segments

Membership is calculated continuously from live data. Customers enter and exit the segment automatically as their behaviour changes. Most segments should be dynamic.

### Static segments

A fixed list of customers captured at a point in time. Use static segments for one-off campaigns where you do not want the audience to change after creation. Create a static segment by clicking **Freeze** on any dynamic segment.

## Combining segments

Create compound segments using set operations:

- **Intersection** — customers who belong to both Segment A AND Segment B
- **Union** — customers who belong to Segment A OR Segment B
- **Exclusion** — customers in Segment A who are NOT in Segment B

<Tip>
Start with broad segments and refine with additional conditions rather than building complex segments from scratch. Previewing at each step helps you understand how each filter narrows the audience.
</Tip>

## Limits

Each workspace can have up to 500 active segments. Individual segments can reference up to 20 filter conditions. If you need more complex logic, use compound segments.
