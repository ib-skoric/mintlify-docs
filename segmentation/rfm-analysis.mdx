RFM analysis scores every customer on three dimensions — recency, frequency, and monetary value — to identify your most valuable customers and those at risk of churning. Luminos AI computes RFM scores automatically from your order history.

## The three dimensions

### Recency (R)

How recently the customer made a purchase. Recent buyers are more engaged and more likely to buy again. Score ranges from 1 (least recent) to 5 (most recent).

### Frequency (F)

How often the customer purchases. Frequent buyers have established a habit and are less likely to churn. Score ranges from 1 (fewest orders) to 5 (most orders).

### Monetary (M)

How much the customer spends in total. High spenders contribute more to revenue even if they buy less frequently. Score ranges from 1 (lowest spend) to 5 (highest spend).

## RFM scoring

Luminos AI divides your customer base into quintiles (5 equal groups) for each dimension. The resulting RFM score is a three-digit number from 111 to 555:

- **555** — Champions: bought recently, buy often, spend the most
- **511** — New customers: bought very recently but only once
- **155** — At-risk high-value: used to buy frequently and spend a lot but have not purchased recently
- **111** — Lost: have not bought recently, bought rarely, and spent little

## Pre-built RFM segments

Luminos AI automatically creates segments for common RFM groupings:

| Segment | RFM scores | Description |
|---------|------------|-------------|
| Champions | 555, 554, 545 | Best customers — reward and engage |
| Loyal | 435, 534, 443 | Regular buyers with moderate spend |
| Promising | 513, 512, 412 | Recent buyers who could become loyal with nurturing |
| At-risk | 254, 255, 154 | Previously valuable customers showing signs of churn |
| Hibernating | 211, 212, 111 | Low engagement across all dimensions |

You can customise these groupings or create additional ones under **Segmentation → RFM → Segments**.

## Using RFM in your strategy

- **Champions** — offer exclusive previews, loyalty rewards, and ask for reviews
- **Loyal** — upsell and cross-sell to increase monetary score
- **Promising** — nurture with onboarding emails and first-purchase follow-ups
- **At-risk** — send re-engagement campaigns with personalised offers
- **Hibernating** — consider win-back discounts or accept natural attrition

<Tip>
Combine RFM segments with [behavioural triggers](/segmentation/behavioural-triggers) to automate your engagement strategy. For example, trigger a win-back email when a Champion's recency score drops below 3.
</Tip>

## Configuration

RFM scores update daily by default. Change the update frequency under **Segmentation → RFM → Settings**. You can also adjust the lookback period — the default is 12 months of order history.
