Lookalike audiences identify potential customers who share characteristics with your best existing customers but have not yet converted. Use them to focus acquisition spend on the prospects most likely to become high-value buyers.

## How it works

Luminos AI builds a profile of your seed audience — typically a high-value segment like "Champions" or "Loyal customers" — by analysing their common attributes:

- Browsing patterns (categories viewed, time on site, visit frequency)
- Geographic distribution
- Device and channel preferences
- Referral sources
- Product affinity signals

The model then scores all non-converting visitors against this profile. Visitors with the highest similarity scores form the lookalike audience.

## Creating a lookalike audience

1. Go to **Segmentation → Lookalike → Create**
2. Select the seed segment (the audience you want to find more of)
3. Set the audience size:
   - **Narrow (1%)** — most similar to the seed, highest conversion likelihood, smallest audience
   - **Medium (5%)** — balanced similarity and reach
   - **Broad (10%)** — wider reach but lower average similarity
4. Preview the audience size and overlap with existing segments
5. Save and activate

## Using lookalike audiences

### On-site personalisation

Apply [recommendation rules](/recommendations/personalisation-rules) to the lookalike audience to show them the same types of products your best customers buy. This can significantly improve first-visit conversion rates.

### Ad targeting

[Sync the lookalike segment](/segmentation/segment-sync-destinations) to Meta Ads or Google Ads for acquisition campaigns. Targeting lookalike audiences typically produces 2–3× better return on ad spend compared to broad interest-based targeting.

### Email campaigns

If lookalike visitors provide their email (e.g. through a newsletter signup), include them in nurture campaigns tailored to their predicted interests.

## Refreshing lookalike audiences

Lookalike models retrain weekly to incorporate new behavioural data. As your seed segment evolves (customers entering and exiting), the lookalike audience adjusts automatically.

<Tip>
Use your highest-value segment as the seed, not just any large segment. A seed of "customers who spent over £500 and purchased 3+ times" produces better lookalikes than "all customers who made a purchase".
</Tip>

## Measuring effectiveness

Track lookalike audience performance under [Segmentation analytics](/segmentation/segmentation-analytics). Compare conversion rates and average order value for lookalike visitors vs. the general visitor population to measure the lift.
