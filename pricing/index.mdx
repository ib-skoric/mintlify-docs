Luminos AI's dynamic pricing module adjusts your product prices automatically based on real-time signals — demand velocity, competitor pricing, inventory levels, and margin targets. The system makes thousands of pricing decisions per day while respecting guardrails you define.

Dynamic pricing is not about racing to the bottom. It is about finding the price point that maximises revenue or margin for each product at each moment, within rules you control.

## Key capabilities

- **Rule-based pricing** — define conditions that trigger price changes
- **Competitor tracking** — monitor rival prices and respond automatically
- **Margin protection** — set floor and ceiling prices to prevent unprofitable or excessive pricing
- **Elasticity modelling** — understand how price changes affect demand
- **Geographic pricing** — vary prices by region or currency
- **Scheduling** — plan time-based price changes for launches and clearances
