The recommendation engine needs three things before it can serve suggestions: a connected product catalog, at least one model, and a placement on your storefront. This guide walks you through each step.

<Note>
Complete the [getting started](/getting-started) guides first — you need a connected store and an active tracking pixel before recommendations can work.
</Note>

<Steps>

<Step title="Verify your product feed">
Go to **Recommendations → Setup → Product feed** and confirm that your catalog is syncing. You should see a count of active products and the last sync timestamp. If products are missing, check your [store connection](/getting-started/connect-your-store) and [catalog import](/getting-started/import-product-catalog) settings.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose a model">
Luminos AI offers several recommendation models:

- **Quick Start** — a pre-trained collaborative filtering model that works with minimal data. Best for new stores.
- **Deep Personalisation** — combines collaborative filtering, content-based matching, and session context. Requires at least 30 days of behavioural data.
- **Trending** — ranks products by recent popularity velocity rather than personalisation. Useful for homepages and category pages.
- **Similar Items** — finds visually and categorically similar products. Best for product detail pages.

Select a model under **Recommendations → Models** and click **Enable**.
</Step>

<Step title="Create a placement">
A placement defines where recommendations appear on your storefront. Go to **Recommendations → Placements → Create** and configure:

- **Name** — a label for internal reference (e.g. "PDP carousel")
- **Page type** — homepage, product detail, cart, checkout, or custom
- **Widget style** — carousel, grid, or list
- **Number of products** — how many items to show (4–20)
- **Model** — which recommendation model powers this placement

Save and publish the placement. The Luminos AI pixel renders the widget automatically on the target page.
</Step>

<Step title="Preview and test">
Open your storefront in a private browser and navigate to the page where you placed the widget. Verify that products appear and that clicking a recommendation logs an event in **Analytics → Real-time**.
</Step>

</Steps>
