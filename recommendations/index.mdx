Luminos AI analyses browsing patterns, purchase history, and product attributes to surface the right products at the right time. The recommendation engine runs continuously, retraining on new data so that suggestions stay relevant as your catalog and customer base evolve.

This section covers everything from initial setup to advanced personalisation rules and performance measurement.

## How it works

The engine combines multiple signals to score every product for every visitor:

- **Collaborative filtering** — what similar shoppers bought or browsed
- **Content-based matching** — product attributes like category, brand, and price range
- **Contextual signals** — time of day, device type, and current cart contents

Scores are computed in real time and served through placements you configure on your storefront — carousels, grids, email blocks, or API responses.
