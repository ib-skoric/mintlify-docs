Cross-selling and upselling are two distinct strategies that work together to increase average order value (AOV). Luminos AI supports both through dedicated models and placement configurations.

## Cross-sell vs. upsell

**Cross-sell** suggests complementary products that pair with what the shopper is viewing or has added to their cart. Examples: a phone case for a phone, running socks for running shoes, or a matching necklace for a dress.

**Upsell** suggests a higher-value alternative to the product the shopper is currently considering. Examples: a larger size, a premium version, or a bundle that includes accessories at a discount.

## Configuring cross-sell

1. Go to **Recommendations → Models** and enable the **Cross-sell** model
2. The model learns product affinities from co-purchase data — items frequently bought together score higher
3. Create a placement on the cart page or product detail page with the cross-sell model selected
4. Set the product count to 4–6 for cart pages and 2–4 for inline suggestions on product pages

### Manual pairings

For products where the algorithm needs help — new launches, accessories with obvious pairings — you can create manual cross-sell rules under **Recommendations → Rules**. Pin the accessory product when the parent product is in view.

## Configuring upsell

1. Enable the **Upsell** model under **Recommendations → Models**
2. The model identifies products in the same category with a higher price point and strong review signals
3. Place upsell widgets on product detail pages, positioned near the add-to-cart button
4. Optionally set a price ceiling — for example, suggest products no more than 50% above the current item's price

## Measuring impact

Track cross-sell and upsell performance separately in [Recommendation analytics](/recommendations/recommendation-analytics):

- **Cross-sell attach rate** — percentage of orders that include a cross-sold product
- **Upsell upgrade rate** — percentage of shoppers who switch to a higher-priced alternative
- **Incremental AOV** — additional revenue per order attributed to cross-sell and upsell widgets

<Tip>
Position cross-sell suggestions on the cart page and upsell suggestions on the product detail page. This aligns with shopper intent — discovery on PDP, completion on cart.
</Tip>

## Avoiding over-promotion

Set frequency caps so that returning visitors do not see the same cross-sell or upsell suggestion on every visit. A cap of 3 impressions per product per visitor per week balances visibility with fatigue.
