Exclusion and suppression rules prevent specific products from appearing in recommendation results. Use them for inventory management, compliance, brand guidelines, or to avoid promoting items that would create a poor customer experience.

## Exclusion vs. suppression

- **Exclusion** completely removes matching products from all recommendation results. The product never appears, regardless of how relevant the model scores it.
- **Suppression** reduces the ranking score so the product appears lower in the list or falls off. It can still appear if no better alternatives exist.

Use exclusion for hard requirements (recalled products, legal restrictions) and suppression for soft preferences (low-margin items, slow-moving inventory).

## Common exclusion patterns

### Out-of-stock products

By default, Luminos AI automatically excludes products with zero inventory. If you want to allow "notify me" or pre-order items to appear, disable the automatic filter under **Recommendations → Settings → Inventory filtering** and create a manual rule that excludes only truly unavailable items.

### Discontinued products

Create an exclusion rule matching the tag `discontinued` or any equivalent status field in your catalog. This prevents stale product pages from being recommended.

### Age-restricted or regulated products

Exclude products in specific categories (alcohol, tobacco, adult content) from placements on pages where age verification has not occurred.

### Recently purchased

Suppress products that the current visitor purchased within the last 30 days to avoid repetitive recommendations. This is especially important for durable goods — no one needs a second washing machine next week.

## Creating an exclusion rule

1. Go to **Recommendations → Rules → Create**
2. Select **Exclude** as the rule type
3. Define the product filter — e.g. `inventory_quantity = 0` or `tags contains "recalled"`
4. Optionally scope to specific placements or time periods
5. Save and publish

## Bulk exclusions

Upload a CSV of product IDs under **Recommendations → Rules → Bulk exclude** to quickly block a large set of products. This is useful after a product recall or vendor change.

<Warning>
Exclusion rules are evaluated before personalisation. Excluding too many products can reduce recommendation quality by shrinking the candidate pool.
</Warning>

## Monitoring

Check the count of excluded and suppressed products under **Recommendations → Rules → Active rules**. If the excluded count exceeds 20% of your catalog, review whether some rules can be converted to suppressions instead.
