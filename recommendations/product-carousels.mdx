Placements define where and how recommendations appear on your storefront. The most common format is a horizontal product carousel, but Luminos AI also supports grids, lists, and custom templates.

## Placement types

### Carousel

A horizontally scrollable row of product cards. Best for product detail pages, homepages, and category pages where screen width is available. Supports 4–20 products with responsive breakpoints.

### Grid

A multi-row grid layout. Effective for dedicated recommendation pages or email-style layouts. Configure the column count (2–6) and total product count.

### List

A vertical list with larger product cards showing descriptions. Works well in sidebars and narrow containers.

### Custom

Use the [JavaScript SDK](/api/sdks/javascript-sdk) or [REST API](/api/rest-api-overview) to fetch recommendation data and render it in your own template. This gives full control over markup and styling.

## Creating a placement

1. Go to **Recommendations → Placements → Create**
2. Enter a name and select the page type (homepage, product detail, cart, checkout, or custom URL)
3. Choose the widget style and number of products
4. Select the recommendation model to power this placement
5. Configure display options — product image size, show/hide price, show/hide ratings
6. Save as draft, preview, then publish

## Page-type recommendations

| Page | Recommended model | Product count | Rationale |
|------|-------------------|---------------|-----------|
| Homepage | Trending or Personalised | 8–12 | Broad discovery for returning and new visitors |
| Product detail | Similar Items | 4–8 | "You might also like" based on current product |
| Cart | Cross-sell | 4–6 | Complementary items that pair with cart contents |
| Checkout | Last-chance upsell | 2–4 | Premium alternatives or accessories before purchase |
| Post-purchase | Replenishment | 4–8 | Items likely to need restocking based on order history |

## Styling

Placements inherit your storefront's CSS by default. Override styles by adding custom CSS classes in the placement settings or by targeting the `.luminos-widget` container in your stylesheet.

<Tip>
Keep carousels above the fold on product detail pages. Recommendations placed below long descriptions get significantly fewer impressions.
</Tip>

## Performance

Each placement loads asynchronously and does not block your page render. Average response time is under 50 milliseconds. Monitor placement performance in [Recommendation analytics](/recommendations/recommendation-analytics).
