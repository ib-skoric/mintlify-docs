The chatbot widget should feel like a natural part of your storefront, not a third-party overlay. Luminos AI provides extensive customisation options so the chat experience matches your brand identity.

## Widget styling

Configure visual settings under **Chatbot → Appearance**:

### Colours

- **Primary colour** — the main accent colour for the chat bubble, send button, and headers
- **Background colour** — the chat window background (light or dark)
- **Text colour** — automatically calculated for contrast, or override manually

### Shape

- **Rounded** — soft corners on the chat window and message bubbles (default)
- **Square** — sharp corners for a more structured look
- **Custom** — set a specific border radius in pixels

### Size

- **Standard** — 380px wide, 520px tall
- **Compact** — 320px wide, 440px tall (better for mobile)
- **Custom** — set exact dimensions

## Chat bubble

The floating button that opens the chat:

- **Icon** — choose from the built-in set (chat bubble, question mark, sparkle) or upload a custom icon
- **Label** — optionally show a text label next to the icon (e.g. "Chat with us")
- **Animation** — subtle bounce or pulse to draw attention (can be disabled)
- **Delay** — how many seconds after page load before the bubble appears (default 2 seconds)

## Bot persona

Give the chatbot a name and avatar:

- **Name** — displayed at the top of the chat window and in message headers (e.g. "Ava", "Lumi", or your brand name)
- **Avatar** — upload a small image (recommended 64×64px). Use a branded icon or illustrated character.

## Conversation styling

- **Message alignment** — bot messages on the left, shopper messages on the right (standard) or custom alignment
- **Typing indicator** — show animated dots while the chatbot is generating a response (recommended for perceived responsiveness)
- **Timestamps** — show or hide message timestamps
- **Product cards** — when the chatbot recommends products, show them as rich cards with image, title, price, and a link button

## Mobile behaviour

On screens narrower than 480px, the chat window expands to fill the screen width. Configure mobile-specific settings under **Chatbot → Appearance → Mobile**:

- Full-screen or bottom-sheet layout
- Whether to show the input field above or below the keyboard
- Touch-friendly button sizes

<Tip>
Test the chatbot appearance on both desktop and mobile devices before going live. Colours that look good on a desktop monitor may have insufficient contrast on mobile screens in direct sunlight.
</Tip>

## Live preview

Every change in the appearance settings is reflected in the live preview panel on the right side of the settings page. You can preview both desktop and mobile layouts without deploying changes to your storefront.
