Chatbot analytics shows how effectively the AI assistant resolves shopper questions and where there are opportunities to improve. Use these metrics to optimise training content, conversation flows, and escalation rules.

## Key metrics

### Resolution rate

The percentage of conversations resolved by the chatbot without human escalation. A healthy resolution rate for ecommerce chatbots is 60–80%. Track this as the primary measure of chatbot effectiveness.

### Escalation rate

The inverse of resolution rate — what percentage of conversations require handoff to a human agent. Break this down by [escalation reason](/chatbot/escalation-to-human) (low confidence, shopper request, sentiment trigger) to identify improvement areas.

### Average response time

How long shoppers wait for a response from the chatbot. AI responses are typically under 2 seconds. If this metric rises, check for backend performance issues.

### Conversations per day

Total conversation volume and trend. Rising volume may indicate growing chatbot adoption or increasing customer issues.

### CSAT score

Average satisfaction rating from [CSAT surveys](/chatbot/csat-collection). Track alongside resolution rate — high resolution with low CSAT means the chatbot is "resolving" conversations without actually satisfying shoppers.

## Dashboard views

Navigate to **Analytics → Chatbot**:

- **Overview** — aggregate metrics and daily trends
- **Topics** — most common conversation topics, resolution rate per topic, and CSAT per topic
- **Flows** — performance of each [conversation flow](/chatbot/conversation-flows) (completion rate, conversion, drop-off)
- **Escalations** — escalation volume, reasons, and post-escalation resolution time
- **Languages** — metrics broken down by [detected language](/chatbot/multilingual-chatbot)
- **Product questions** — which products generate the most chatbot questions (indicates gaps in product descriptions or FAQs)

## Topic analysis

Luminos AI automatically clusters conversations by topic using NLP. The topic view shows:

- Topic name (auto-generated, editable)
- Conversation volume
- Resolution rate
- Average CSAT
- Trend (increasing or decreasing)

Low-resolution topics are opportunities to add knowledge base content or create a dedicated [conversation flow](/chatbot/conversation-flows).

## Conversation review

Drill into individual conversations under **Chatbot → Conversations**. Each transcript shows the full dialogue, the chatbot's internal confidence scores, knowledge sources used, and whether escalation occurred. Use the transcript review to identify training improvements.

<Tip>
Schedule a weekly 15-minute chatbot review. Read the 10 lowest-CSAT conversations and the 5 most-escalated topics. Make targeted knowledge base updates based on what you find.
</Tip>
