The Luminos AI Chatbot is a conversational AI assistant that sits on your storefront and helps shoppers find products, check order status, initiate returns, and get answers to common questions — all without waiting for a human agent.

The chatbot learns from your product catalog, knowledge base, and order data to provide accurate, contextual responses. When it cannot resolve an issue, it escalates seamlessly to your support team.

## Key capabilities

- **Product discovery** — help shoppers find what they are looking for through conversation
- **Order tracking** — let customers check delivery status and shipping updates
- **Returns and exchanges** — automate return initiation and exchange workflows
- **FAQ resolution** — answer common questions from your knowledge base
- **Multilingual** — detect and respond in the shopper's preferred language
- **Brand customisation** — match the widget to your visual identity
