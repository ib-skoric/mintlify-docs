Customer satisfaction (CSAT) surveys measure how well the chatbot resolves shopper needs. Luminos AI can prompt shoppers for a rating and optional comment after each conversation, giving you a continuous feedback loop.

## Enabling CSAT collection

Go to **Chatbot → CSAT → Enable**. Configure when the survey appears:

- **After resolution** — the chatbot asks for a rating after it determines the conversation is resolved (no messages for 2 minutes)
- **After escalation** — collect a rating after the conversation transfers to a human agent and is resolved
- **After specific flows** — trigger a survey at the end of [conversation flows](/chatbot/conversation-flows) like order status or returns

## Survey format

The default survey has two parts:

### Rating

A 1–5 star rating or thumbs up/down. Choose the format under **Chatbot → CSAT → Survey format**:

- **Stars** — 1 to 5 stars, providing more granularity
- **Thumbs** — binary thumbs up or down, yielding higher response rates due to simplicity

### Comment

An optional free-text field where the shopper can explain their rating. Comments provide qualitative context that star ratings alone do not capture.

## Survey placement

The survey appears as the last message in the chat window. It does not open a new window or redirect the shopper. This inline approach achieves response rates of 15–25%, significantly higher than post-session email surveys.

## Analysing results

View CSAT data under **Chatbot → CSAT → Dashboard**:

- **Overall CSAT score** — average rating across all surveyed conversations
- **CSAT trend** — score over time (daily, weekly, monthly)
- **CSAT by topic** — which conversation topics get the best and worst ratings
- **CSAT by flow** — performance of each conversation flow
- **Low-rated conversations** — drill into conversations with 1–2 star ratings to understand what went wrong

## Feedback loop

Use low-rated conversations to improve the chatbot:

1. Filter for conversations with 1–2 star ratings
2. Read the transcript to identify what the chatbot got wrong
3. Add or update knowledge base content, adjust conversation flows, or refine escalation rules
4. Track whether CSAT improves in subsequent weeks

<Tip>
Aim for a CSAT score above 4.0 (out of 5.0). Scores below 3.5 indicate systemic issues with answer quality or escalation handling that need priority attention.
</Tip>

## Privacy

CSAT responses are stored in Luminos AI and included in [data retention](/account/data-retention) policies. Shopper comments may contain personal information — ensure your retention settings comply with [GDPR requirements](/account/gdpr-compliance).
