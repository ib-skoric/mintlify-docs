Deploying the Luminos AI chatbot takes a few minutes. The widget loads asynchronously, so it does not affect your page load performance.

<Steps>

<Step title="Enable the chatbot">
Go to **Chatbot → Settings → Enable**. This activates the chatbot backend and starts indexing your product catalog for conversational product discovery.
</Step>

<Step title="Install the widget">
Copy the widget snippet from **Chatbot → Settings → Install**:

```html
<script>
  (function(l,u,m){var c=u.createElement('script');c.async=1;
  c.src='https://cdn.luminos.ai/chatbot.js';c.dataset.workspace=m;
  u.body.appendChild(c)})(window,document,'YOUR_WORKSPACE_ID');
</script>
```

Paste it before the closing `</body>` tag on your storefront. If you already have the [tracking pixel](/getting-started/install-tracking-pixel) installed, the chatbot widget loads alongside it.
</Step>

<Step title="Configure placement">
By default, the chatbot appears as a floating button in the bottom-right corner. You can change the position under **Chatbot → Settings → Widget position**:

- **Bottom-right** (default)
- **Bottom-left**
- **Custom** — specify exact coordinates or attach to a specific element
</Step>

<Step title="Set the greeting">
Configure the welcome message shoppers see when they open the chat:

- **Default greeting** — "Hi! How can I help you today?"
- **Page-aware greeting** — the chatbot adapts its greeting based on the page type (e.g. "Looking for help with this product?" on product pages)
- **Custom greeting** — write your own message

Configure greetings under **Chatbot → Settings → Greeting**.
</Step>

<Step title="Test the deployment">
Visit your storefront and click the chatbot icon. Try asking about a product, checking an order status, and asking a general question. Verify responses in the **Chatbot → Conversations** log.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Page targeting

By default, the chatbot appears on every page. To limit it to specific pages:

1. Go to **Chatbot → Settings → Page targeting**
2. Add URL patterns where the chatbot should appear (e.g. `/products/*`, `/support/*`)
3. Or add exclusion patterns where it should not appear (e.g. `/checkout/*` to avoid distracting during purchase)

<Tip>
Deploy the chatbot on product pages and support pages first. These are the highest-value locations where shoppers have the most questions.
</Tip>
