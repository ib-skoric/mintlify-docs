Not every conversation can be resolved by AI. Luminos AI provides configurable escalation paths so that shoppers reach a human agent when they need one, with full context from the chatbot conversation.

## Escalation triggers

Configure when the chatbot should escalate under **Chatbot → Escalation → Rules**:

### Automatic triggers

- **Low confidence** — the chatbot's answer confidence is below a threshold (default 40%). The chatbot says something like "I'm not sure about this — let me connect you with someone who can help."
- **Sentiment detection** — the shopper's messages indicate frustration or anger. Luminos AI's sentiment model detects negative language patterns.
- **Repeated questions** — the shopper asks the same question multiple times, indicating the chatbot is not providing a satisfactory answer.
- **Topic-based** — certain topics always escalate (e.g. billing disputes, complaints, legal questions).

### Manual trigger

The chatbot always offers a "Talk to a person" option. Shoppers can request a human at any point in the conversation.

## Escalation destinations

Choose where escalated conversations go:

- **Live chat** — transfer to your live chat platform (Intercom, Zendesk, Freshdesk, or custom via webhook)
- **Email** — create a support ticket with the conversation transcript
- **Phone** — display a phone number for the shopper to call
- **Callback** — collect the shopper's phone number and queue a callback from your team

Configure destinations under **Chatbot → Escalation → Destinations**.

## Context handoff

When escalating, Luminos AI sends the full conversation transcript and extracted context to the human agent:

- Conversation history (all messages)
- Shopper profile (if known) — name, email, order history
- Current page URL and products being viewed
- Chatbot's internal notes — what it tried, what failed, what the shopper is asking about

This context eliminates the need for the shopper to repeat themselves.

## Availability-aware escalation

If your support team is offline, configure fallback behaviour:

- **Queue** — hold the escalation and notify the shopper of expected wait time
- **Email** — create a ticket instead of a live handoff
- **Scheduled callback** — offer the shopper a callback slot during business hours
- **Continue with chatbot** — explain that humans are unavailable and continue the AI conversation

Set business hours under **Chatbot → Escalation → Availability**.

<Tip>
Review escalated conversations weekly to identify topics the chatbot should handle better. Each unnecessary escalation is an opportunity to improve the chatbot's training data.
</Tip>

## Monitoring

Track escalation metrics in [Chatbot analytics](/chatbot/chatbot-analytics) → **Escalations**: escalation rate, reasons, resolution time after handoff, and [CSAT scores](/chatbot/csat-collection) for escalated vs. non-escalated conversations.
