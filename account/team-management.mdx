Team management lets you control who has access to your Luminos AI workspace and what they can do. Invite team members by email, organise them into teams, and assign roles that match their responsibilities.

## Inviting team members

1. Go to **Settings → Team → Invite**
2. Enter one or more email addresses
3. Select a [role](/account/roles-permissions) for the invited members
4. Click **Send invitations**

Invited users receive an email with a link to join your workspace. Invitations expire after 7 days. Resend expired invitations from **Settings → Team → Pending**.

## Managing members

View all active members under **Settings → Team → Members**. For each member you can:

- **Change role** — adjust their permissions (requires Admin role)
- **Deactivate** — remove their access without deleting their account. Deactivated members can be reactivated later.
- **Remove** — permanently remove them from the workspace

## Teams

Organise members into teams for easier management:

1. Go to **Settings → Team → Teams → Create**
2. Name the team (e.g. "Marketing", "Engineering", "Support")
3. Add members to the team
4. Optionally assign a team-level default role

Teams are used for:

- **Report sharing** — share [dashboards](/analytics/dashboards-overview) with a team instead of listing individual emails
- **Alert routing** — send [inventory alerts](/inventory/low-stock-alerts) or chatbot escalations to a team channel
- **Access scoping** — restrict certain modules or data to specific teams (Enterprise plan feature)

## Member limits

| Plan | Member limit |
|------|-------------|
| Starter | 5 |
| Growth | 25 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

If you need more members than your plan allows, [upgrade your plan](/account/billing-plans) or contact [support](/account/contact-support) for a custom arrangement.

<Tip>
Use teams to mirror your organisational structure. When someone changes roles internally, updating their team membership automatically adjusts their access to the right dashboards, reports, and alerts.
</Tip>

## Activity

View a log of team membership changes under **Settings → Team → Activity**. This shows who was invited, removed, or had their role changed, and by whom.
