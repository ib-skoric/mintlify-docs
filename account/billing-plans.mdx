## Changing your plan

### Upgrading

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Change plan**
2. Select the new plan
3. Review the prorated charge for the remainder of the current billing period
4. Confirm the upgrade

Upgrades take effect immediately. New limits and features are available right away.

### Downgrading

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Change plan**
2. Select the lower plan
3. Review any features that will be disabled and data that may be affected (e.g. excess team members will be deactivated)
4. Confirm the downgrade

Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Payment methods

Luminos AI accepts credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and bank transfers (Enterprise only). Manage payment methods under **Settings → Billing → Payment methods**.

## Invoices

View and download invoices under **Settings → Billing → Invoices**. Invoices are generated monthly and include a detailed breakdown of charges.

## Annual billing

Save 20% by switching to annual billing. Go to **Settings → Billing → Billing cycle → Switch to annual**.

<Tip>
Start with the Starter plan and upgrade when you approach limits. There is no penalty for upgrading mid-cycle, and the prorated pricing means you only pay the difference.
</Tip>
