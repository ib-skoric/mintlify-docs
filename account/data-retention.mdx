Luminos AI retains different types of data for different periods. You can customise retention periods to meet your compliance requirements and storage preferences.

## Default retention periods

| Data type | Default retention | Configurable range |
|-----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Behavioural events (page views, clicks) | 12 months | 1–36 months |
| Purchase events | 36 months | 12–84 months |
| Product catalog (current) | Indefinite | N/A |
| Product catalog (historical versions) | 6 months | 1–24 months |
| Recommendation logs | 6 months | 1–12 months |
| Search query logs | 6 months | 1–12 months |
| Chatbot transcripts | 12 months | 1–36 months |
| Analytics aggregates | 36 months | 12–84 months |
| Audit logs | 12 months | 12–84 months |
| Customer profiles | Until deletion requested | N/A |

## Configuring retention

1. Go to **Settings → Data → Retention**
2. Adjust the retention period for each data type
3. Save — changes take effect within 24 hours

When the retention period expires, data is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

<Warning>
Shortening retention periods triggers irreversible data deletion. Ensure you have exported any data you need before reducing retention windows.
</Warning>

## Impact on AI features

Shorter retention periods affect AI model quality:

- **Recommendations** — collaborative filtering needs at least 6 months of behavioural data for accurate personalisation. Reducing event retention below 6 months degrades recommendation quality.
- **Forecasting** — demand prediction models need at least 12 months of sales data for seasonal pattern detection. Reducing purchase event retention below 12 months eliminates seasonal learning.
- **Segmentation** — RFM scores and lifecycle stages use the full range of available purchase history. Shorter retention compresses the data window.

## Data deletion requests

For GDPR compliance, you can delete all data for a specific customer. See [GDPR and privacy compliance](/account/gdpr-compliance) for details on processing data subject deletion requests.

## Data export before deletion

Before data reaches the end of its retention period, you can [export it](/analytics/export-data) to your own data warehouse for long-term storage. Set up a [warehouse sync](/analytics/export-data) to automatically push data to Snowflake, BigQuery, or Redshift before it expires.

<Tip>
Set up warehouse sync before reducing retention periods. This preserves historical data in your own infrastructure while keeping Luminos AI storage lean.
</Tip>
