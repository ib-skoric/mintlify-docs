The Luminos AI support team is here to help you with technical issues, configuration questions, and account management. Choose the channel that matches the urgency of your request.

## Support channels

### Live chat

Click the chat icon in the bottom-right corner of the Luminos AI dashboard to start a conversation with the support team. Live chat is the fastest way to get help for non-critical issues.

**Availability:** Monday to Friday, 9:00–18:00 (your workspace timezone)

### Email

Send an email to support@luminos.ai with a description of your issue. Include your workspace ID (found under **Settings → General → Workspace ID**) to help us locate your account quickly.

### Phone

Enterprise customers have access to phone support. Your dedicated account manager provides the direct number during onboarding.

## Response time SLAs

| Priority | Starter | Growth | Enterprise |
|----------|---------|--------|------------|
| **Critical** (platform down) | 4 hours | 1 hour | 15 minutes |
| **High** (feature broken) | 24 hours | 8 hours | 2 hours |
| **Normal** (question/guidance) | 48 hours | 24 hours | 8 hours |
| **Low** (feature request) | 5 business days | 3 business days | 1 business day |

## What to include in a support request

To help us resolve your issue quickly, include:

- **Workspace ID** — found under **Settings → General**
- **Description** — what you expected to happen and what actually happened
- **Steps to reproduce** — how to recreate the issue
- **Screenshots or screen recordings** — visual evidence of the problem
- **Error messages** — any error codes or messages you see
- **Urgency** — how this impacts your business operations

## Status page

Check the current platform status and subscribe to incident notifications at **status.luminos.ai**. The status page shows uptime for all major components:

- Dashboard and API
- Recommendation engine
- Smart Search
- Chatbot
- Data pipeline
- Tracking pixel

## Feature requests

Submit feature requests through the support channels above. We review and prioritise requests based on customer demand and strategic alignment. You can track the status of your requests under **Settings → Support → Requests**.
