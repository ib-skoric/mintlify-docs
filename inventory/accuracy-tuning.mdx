Accurate forecasts are the foundation of effective inventory management. Luminos AI tracks forecast accuracy continuously and provides tools to identify and correct sources of error.

## Accuracy metrics

### MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error)

The primary accuracy metric. MAPE measures the average percentage difference between predicted and actual demand. Lower is better.

| MAPE range | Rating |
|------------|--------|
| Under 15% | Excellent |
| 15–25% | Good |
| 25–40% | Needs improvement |
| Over 40% | Poor — investigate data quality |

### Bias

Bias measures whether forecasts consistently over-predict or under-predict demand. A positive bias means the model predicts more than actual demand (overstock risk). A negative bias means it predicts less (stockout risk).

### Weighted MAPE

Standard MAPE gives equal weight to every product. Weighted MAPE weights products by revenue contribution, so accuracy on your best sellers matters more than accuracy on slow movers.

## Viewing accuracy reports

Go to **Inventory → Analytics → Forecast accuracy**. The dashboard shows:

- Overall MAPE across your catalog
- MAPE by product category
- MAPE trend over time (is accuracy improving or degrading?)
- Top products with the highest forecast error
- Bias distribution across the catalog

## Improving accuracy

### Fix data quality issues

The most common cause of poor accuracy is bad input data. Check:

- **Missing sales data** — orders not syncing from your store
- **Duplicate events** — double-counted sales inflating history
- **Uncategorised products** — products without categories cannot benefit from cross-product learning

### Add demand signals

Enable additional [demand signals](/inventory/demand-signals) like marketing calendars and traffic data. External signals help the model anticipate demand changes rather than reacting to them.

### Create seasonal events

If accuracy drops around holidays or promotions, add [seasonal events](/inventory/seasonal-adjustments) so the model knows to expect abnormal demand.

### Adjust safety stock

If forecast bias is consistently negative (under-prediction), increase the safety stock buffer under **Inventory → Settings → Safety stock**. This adds a buffer to reorder points to compensate for systematic under-prediction.

<Tip>
Focus accuracy improvement efforts on your top 50 products by revenue. Improving forecasts for high-value SKUs has the biggest impact on stockout prevention and working capital.
</Tip>

## Feedback loop

When you override a forecast or adjust a reorder quantity manually, Luminos AI records the adjustment as feedback. Over time, the model learns from these corrections and adjusts its predictions accordingly.
