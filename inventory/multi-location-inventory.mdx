If you fulfil orders from multiple locations — warehouses, retail stores, or 3PL partners — Luminos AI generates separate forecasts for each location based on its unique demand patterns and geographic customer base.

## Setting up locations

1. Go to **Inventory → Locations → Add**
2. Enter the location name, address, and type (warehouse, retail store, 3PL, or drop-ship)
3. Map the location to your [warehouse integration](/inventory/warehouse-integrations) so stock levels sync automatically
4. Set the fulfilment priority — which location ships first when inventory is available in multiple places

## Location-level forecasts

Each location receives its own demand forecast based on:

- **Historical demand** — sales fulfilled from that specific location
- **Geographic demand** — customer proximity and shipping zone preferences
- **Channel mix** — online vs. in-store demand varies by location

View location-level forecasts under **Inventory → Forecasts** and filter by location.

## Inventory allocation

When a product is available in multiple locations, Luminos AI recommends how to allocate incoming stock across locations. The allocation model considers:

- **Predicted demand** at each location for the next replenishment cycle
- **Current stock** — locations with lower stock relative to predicted demand get more units
- **Shipping costs** — the model favours placing inventory closer to predicted demand centres
- **Storage capacity** — respects maximum capacity constraints per location

View allocation recommendations under **Inventory → Allocation → Suggestions**.

## Transfer recommendations

When stock is imbalanced — one location has excess while another is running low — Luminos AI suggests inter-location transfers. Transfers factor in shipping time, cost, and whether the excess location has enough buffer to cover its own demand during the transfer window.

Review transfer suggestions under **Inventory → Transfers → Suggested**.

<Tip>
Set accurate shipping times between your locations so transfer recommendations account for transit delays. Overly optimistic transit times can lead to stockouts at the receiving location.
</Tip>

## Consolidated view

The **Inventory → Overview** dashboard shows aggregated stock across all locations, with the ability to drill down into any specific location. Colour-coded indicators flag locations where stock is low relative to forecast demand.

## Location-specific alerts

[Low stock alerts](/inventory/low-stock-alerts) can be configured per location. A product might have healthy overall stock but be dangerously low at a specific fulfilment centre. Location-specific alerts catch these imbalances.
