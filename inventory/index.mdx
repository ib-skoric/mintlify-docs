Luminos AI's inventory forecasting module uses machine learning to predict future demand for every product in your catalog. Accurate forecasts help you order the right quantities at the right time — avoiding both stockouts that lose sales and overstock that ties up capital.

The module ingests sales history, traffic patterns, marketing calendars, and external signals to produce daily and weekly demand forecasts at the SKU level.

## Key capabilities

- **SKU-level forecasts** — predictions for every product variant, not just aggregates
- **Multi-model ensemble** — combines statistical and ML models for higher accuracy
- **Alert system** — notifications when predicted stock drops below reorder thresholds
- **Reorder automation** — auto-generate purchase orders based on forecast outputs
- **Multi-location support** — separate forecasts for each warehouse or fulfilment centre
