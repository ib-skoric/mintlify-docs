Luminos AI uses an ensemble of forecasting models to predict demand. Each model has different strengths, and the ensemble selects the best-performing approach for each product based on historical accuracy.

## Available models

### Exponential smoothing (ETS)

A statistical model that captures level, trend, and seasonality from historical sales data. ETS works well for products with stable, predictable demand patterns and is fast to compute.

### ARIMA

Auto-regressive integrated moving average models that capture complex temporal dependencies. ARIMA adapts to shifting demand patterns better than ETS but requires more historical data — at least 90 days of sales.

### Gradient-boosted trees (GBT)

A machine learning model that incorporates non-time-series features like marketing spend, traffic volume, pricing changes, and competitor activity. GBT excels at capturing the impact of external events on demand.

### Deep learning (DeepAR)

A neural network architecture designed for probabilistic forecasting. DeepAR learns patterns across your entire catalog simultaneously, so insights from high-volume products improve forecasts for slower-moving items. It requires at least 180 days of data to train effectively.

## Model selection

Luminos AI runs all available models in parallel and evaluates their accuracy over the most recent 30-day validation window. For each product, the model with the lowest MAPE (mean absolute percentage error) is selected for production forecasts.

You can view which model was selected for any product under **Inventory → Forecasts → Product detail → Model info**.

## Manual overrides

If you know that a specific model works better for a category of products — for example, ETS for consumables with stable demand — you can pin a model under **Inventory → Settings → Model overrides**. Pinned models bypass automatic selection.

<Tip>
Let the ensemble run for at least 60 days before overriding model selection. The automatic approach needs enough validation cycles to reliably pick the best model.
</Tip>

## Forecast horizon

Forecasts are generated for three horizons:

- **7 days** — short-term planning for daily replenishment
- **30 days** — mid-term planning for regular purchase orders
- **90 days** — long-term planning for seasonal buying and budget allocation

All three horizons update daily with the latest data.
