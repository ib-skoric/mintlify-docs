Low stock alerts notify you when a product's inventory is likely to run out before your next replenishment arrives. Luminos AI offers two alert types: threshold-based alerts that trigger at a fixed stock level, and predictive alerts that use demand forecasts to estimate when stockout will occur.

## Threshold-based alerts

Set a minimum stock level per product or category. When inventory drops below the threshold, Luminos AI sends an alert.

1. Go to **Inventory → Alerts → Thresholds**
2. Set thresholds at the product, category, or global level
3. Global thresholds apply to all products unless overridden by a more specific rule

**Example:** Set a global threshold of 10 units and a category-specific threshold of 50 units for your bestselling category.

## Predictive alerts

Predictive alerts are more proactive. They combine current stock levels with demand forecasts and supplier lead times to estimate a stockout date. An alert fires when the estimated stockout date falls within your configured warning window.

1. Go to **Inventory → Alerts → Predictive**
2. Set the warning window — how many days before predicted stockout you want to be alerted (default 14 days)
3. Enter supplier lead times per product or supplier. If lead time is not set, Luminos AI uses a default of 7 days.

**Example:** A product has 200 units in stock, predicted demand of 20 units per day, and a supplier lead time of 5 days. The predicted stockout is in 10 days. With a 14-day warning window, the alert fires immediately because you need to reorder now to receive stock in time.

## Alert channels

Configure where alerts are delivered under **Inventory → Alerts → Channels**:

- **Email** — send alerts to one or more team members
- **Slack** — post alerts to a Slack channel via webhook
- **Dashboard** — alerts appear in the Luminos AI notification centre
- **Webhook** — send alert payloads to your own systems via the [webhooks API](/api/webhooks)

## Alert frequency

To avoid alert fatigue, configure the minimum interval between repeated alerts for the same product. The default is 24 hours — if a product remains below threshold, you receive a reminder once per day rather than continuously.

<Tip>
Predictive alerts are more valuable than threshold alerts for products with variable demand. A fixed threshold of 50 units might be too high for slow-moving products and too low for fast-moving ones.
</Tip>

## Reviewing alerts

View all active and recent alerts under **Inventory → Alerts → History**. Each alert shows the product, current stock level, predicted demand, and recommended reorder quantity. You can dismiss alerts, mark them as actioned, or create a [reorder](/inventory/reorder-automation) directly from the alert.
