The Luminos AI dashboard is your daily operational view. It shows key performance indicators across all AI modules on a single screen, with the ability to drill into any metric for deeper analysis.

## Default dashboard

When you open **Analytics → Dashboard**, you see the default overview with these widgets:

### Revenue summary

- **Total revenue** — all orders in the selected period
- **AI-influenced revenue** — orders that involved a recommendation click, search interaction, or chatbot-assisted discovery
- **AI influence rate** — percentage of total revenue that AI features contributed to

### Module performance

- **Recommendation CTR** — click-through rate on recommendation widgets
- **Search conversion** — percentage of search sessions resulting in a purchase
- **Chatbot resolution rate** — percentage of chatbot conversations resolved without human escalation
- **Inventory health** — percentage of products with adequate stock levels

### Trends

Line charts showing daily trends for each module's primary metric over the last 30 days. Hover over data points for exact values.

## Date range

Use the date picker in the top-right corner to change the reporting period. Options include:

- Today, Yesterday, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days
- Month to date, Quarter to date, Year to date
- Custom range with start and end dates

Comparison mode lets you overlay the previous period to spot trends.

## Custom dashboards

Create custom dashboards under **Analytics → Dashboards → Create**. Drag widgets from the widget library and arrange them in a grid layout. Available widgets include:

- Metric cards (single number with trend arrow)
- Line charts, bar charts, and pie charts
- Tables with sortable columns
- Funnel visualisations
- Real-time counters

Save multiple dashboards for different audiences — one for the marketing team, another for operations, and a technical dashboard for developers.

## Sharing

Share dashboards with team members via a direct link. Recipients need a Luminos AI account with at least [Viewer permissions](/account/roles-permissions). For stakeholders without accounts, [schedule a report](/analytics/scheduled-reports) instead.

<Tip>
Pin your most important dashboard as the landing page under **Settings → Preferences → Default dashboard**. This ensures your team sees the most relevant metrics first.
</Tip>
