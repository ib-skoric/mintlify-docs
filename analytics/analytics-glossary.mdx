This glossary defines the key terms, metrics, and concepts used across Luminos AI analytics.

## Metrics

### Add-to-cart rate

The percentage of product views that result in the product being added to the shopping cart.

### Average order value (AOV)

Total revenue divided by the number of orders. A higher AOV indicates that shoppers are purchasing more or higher-priced items per transaction.

### Click-through rate (CTR)

The percentage of impressions (recommendation views, search results, or chatbot suggestions) that result in a click. Calculated as clicks divided by impressions.

### Conversion rate

The percentage of sessions (or a specific audience) that complete a purchase. Can be calculated at the session level, search level, recommendation level, or chatbot level depending on context.

### Customer lifetime value (LTV)

The total revenue attributed to a customer over their entire relationship with your store. Luminos AI calculates observed LTV (based on actual purchases) and predicted LTV (using the [predictive scoring](/segmentation/predictive-scoring) model).

### MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error)

A forecast accuracy metric used in [inventory analytics](/inventory/inventory-analytics). The average percentage difference between predicted and actual demand. Lower values indicate more accurate forecasts.

### Resolution rate

The percentage of chatbot conversations that are resolved without [escalation to a human agent](/chatbot/escalation-to-human).

### Revenue per impression (RPI)

Revenue attributed to a feature divided by the total number of impressions. Used to compare the revenue efficiency of different recommendation placements.

### Stockout rate

The percentage of products with zero available inventory. Tracked in [inventory analytics](/inventory/inventory-analytics).

### Zero-result rate

The percentage of search queries that return no products. Tracked in [search analytics](/search/search-analytics).

## Dimensions

### Cohort

A group of customers who share a common characteristic, typically the date of first purchase. Used in [cohort analysis](/analytics/cohort-analysis).

### Segment

A dynamically defined group of customers based on behaviour, value, or demographics. See [customer segmentation](/segmentation).

### Touchpoint

An interaction between a shopper and an AI feature — a recommendation click, search result click, or chatbot message. Touchpoints are the basis of [revenue attribution](/analytics/revenue-attribution).

## Attribution models

### Last-touch

Revenue is attributed entirely to the last AI touchpoint before purchase. The default model in Luminos AI.

### Linear

Revenue is split equally across all touchpoints in the shopper's journey.

### Time decay

Touchpoints closer to the purchase receive more credit than earlier touchpoints.

### Position-based

40% credit to the first touchpoint, 40% to the last, and 20% distributed among middle touchpoints.
