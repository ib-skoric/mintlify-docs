Scheduled reports deliver analytics data to your team automatically on a recurring basis. Set them up once and stop worrying about manually pulling numbers for weekly reviews or monthly stakeholder updates.

## Creating a scheduled report

1. Go to **Analytics → Reports** and open a saved report (or [create a new one](/analytics/custom-reports))
2. Click **Schedule** in the report toolbar
3. Configure the schedule:
   - **Frequency** — daily, weekly, monthly, or custom cron expression
   - **Day** — for weekly schedules, select the day (e.g. Monday for weekly reviews)
   - **Time** — when the report runs (in your workspace timezone)
   - **Date range** — the relative period the report covers (e.g. "Last 7 days" recalculates each run)
4. Choose the delivery channel:
   - **Email** — enter one or more email addresses. The report arrives as a PDF attachment with a CSV data file.
   - **Slack** — select a channel or DM. The report posts a summary with key metrics and a link to the full report in Luminos AI.
5. Save the schedule

## Managing schedules

View all active schedules under **Analytics → Scheduled → Active**. Each entry shows:

- Report name
- Schedule (frequency, day, time)
- Delivery channel and recipients
- Last run status (success, failed, skipped)
- Next run time

Edit, pause, or delete schedules from this view.

## Common scheduled reports

| Report | Frequency | Audience | Purpose |
|--------|-----------|----------|---------|
| Weekly AI performance | Monday 9:00 | Marketing team | CTR, conversion, revenue by module |
| Daily search health | Daily 8:00 | Merchandising | Zero-result queries, top queries |
| Monthly pricing review | 1st of month | Finance | Margin trends, competitive position |
| Weekly chatbot review | Monday 9:00 | Support lead | Resolution rate, escalation topics |
| Inventory alerts digest | Daily 7:00 | Operations | Products at risk of stockout |

## Troubleshooting

If a scheduled report fails to deliver:

- **Email** — check that recipient email addresses are valid. Reports over 25 MB are not sent; reduce the date range or filter to decrease size.
- **Slack** — verify the Slack webhook URL under **Settings → Integrations → Slack**. The webhook may have been revoked if the Slack app was reinstalled.

<Tip>
Schedule reports to arrive before your regular meetings. A weekly AI performance report delivered at 8:00 AM Monday gives you time to review before the 10:00 AM team standup.
</Tip>
