Luminos AI's analytics module gives you a unified view of how AI features impact your business. Every recommendation click, search query, chatbot conversation, pricing change, and inventory decision is tracked and connected to revenue outcomes.

The module is designed for both daily monitoring and deep analysis — real-time dashboards for operational awareness and custom reports for strategic decisions.

## Key capabilities

- **Cross-module dashboards** — see recommendations, search, chatbot, pricing, and inventory metrics in one place
- **Revenue attribution** — understand which AI features drive revenue and by how much
- **Custom reports** — build tailored analyses with drag-and-drop dimensions and measures
- **Funnel and cohort analysis** — track shopper journeys and customer group behaviour over time
- **Scheduled delivery** — automate report distribution via email and Slack
- **Data export** — download data for analysis in external tools
