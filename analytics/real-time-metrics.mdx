The real-time metrics dashboard shows what is happening on your storefront right now. Use it during product launches, flash sales, and high-traffic events to monitor performance and catch issues as they happen.

## Real-time dashboard

Navigate to **Analytics → Real-time**. The dashboard updates every 5 seconds with:

### Active visitors

The number of shoppers currently browsing your storefront. A sparkline shows the last 30 minutes of visitor count.

### Events per second

The rate of behavioural events flowing into Luminos AI — page views, product views, add-to-cart, and purchases. Use this to verify that the [tracking pixel](/getting-started/install-tracking-pixel) is functioning during peak traffic.

### Live conversions

Purchase events as they happen, with product name, order value, and the AI feature that influenced the purchase (if any).

### Module health

Status indicators for each AI module:

- **Recommendations** — response latency (green: <100ms, yellow: 100–500ms, red: >500ms)
- **Search** — query latency and error rate
- **Chatbot** — active conversations and resolution rate
- **Pricing** — last price sync timestamp

## Event stream

The event stream shows individual events as they arrive:

- **Type** — page view, product view, add to cart, purchase, search, recommendation click
- **Visitor** — anonymous ID or customer name (if logged in)
- **Page** — the URL where the event occurred
- **Product** — the product involved (if applicable)
- **Timestamp** — event time

Filter the stream by event type, product category, or segment to focus on what matters.

## Alerts

Configure real-time alerts under **Analytics → Real-time → Alerts**:

- **Traffic drop** — alert if visitor count drops more than 50% in 5 minutes (may indicate a site outage)
- **Event gap** — alert if no events are received for 2 minutes (tracking pixel may be broken)
- **Error spike** — alert if recommendation or search error rates exceed 5%
- **Conversion anomaly** — alert if conversion rate drops below a threshold

Alerts are delivered via email, Slack, or the Luminos AI notification centre.

<Tip>
Keep the real-time dashboard open during Black Friday, product launches, and marketing campaign go-lives. Real-time visibility lets you catch and resolve issues in minutes rather than discovering them in the next day's reports.
</Tip>
