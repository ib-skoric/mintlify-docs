Search analytics reveals how shoppers use search on your storefront and where the experience falls short. Use these insights to add synonyms, tune relevance, create merchandising rules, and expand your catalog.

## Key metrics

### Search usage rate

The percentage of all sessions that include at least one search. Typical ecommerce stores see 15–30%. Higher rates indicate shoppers rely on search for navigation.

### Click-through rate

The percentage of search results pages where the shopper clicks at least one product. Low CTR suggests that results are not relevant to the query.

### Zero-result rate

The percentage of searches that return no products. Each zero-result query is a missed sale opportunity. Target a zero-result rate below 5%.

### Search conversion rate

The percentage of search sessions that end in a purchase. Compare this to overall site conversion to understand the value of search traffic.

### Revenue per search

Total revenue from sessions that included search, divided by the number of searches. This metric captures the full value of search, including purchases that happen after the shopper navigates away from the results page.

## Reports

### Top queries

View the most searched terms ranked by volume. Each query shows its CTR, conversion rate, and average result count. Use this to identify your most important queries and ensure they return excellent results.

### Zero-result queries

A list of queries that returned no products, ranked by frequency. For each, consider whether you need to:

- Add a [synonym](/search/synonyms-redirects) mapping the term to products you carry
- Add a [redirect](/search/synonyms-redirects) to a relevant landing page
- Expand your catalog to cover the demand

### Low-click queries

Queries with impressions but unusually low CTR. These suggest that the results are not matching shopper intent. Test the query in the **Search → Preview** tool to diagnose relevance issues.

### Facet usage

Which [faceted filters](/search/faceted-filtering) shoppers use most, and which filter combinations lead to purchases. Use this to prioritise facet ordering and identify popular attribute values.

## Exports

Export any report as CSV under the download icon. For recurring reports, create a [scheduled report](/analytics/scheduled-reports) that delivers search analytics to your inbox weekly.

<Tip>
Review zero-result queries weekly. They are the fastest signal for gaps in your catalog or synonym list.
</Tip>
