Visual search lets shoppers upload a photo — from their camera, screenshots, or saved images — and find visually similar products in your catalog. Luminos AI uses a deep learning model to extract visual features like colour, shape, pattern, and style, then matches them against your product images.

## How it works

1. The shopper clicks the camera icon in the search bar and uploads an image
2. Luminos AI's vision model extracts a feature embedding from the image
3. The embedding is compared against pre-computed embeddings for every product image in your catalog
4. The most visually similar products are returned as search results, ranked by similarity score

The entire process takes under 500 milliseconds for catalogs up to 500,000 images.

## Enabling visual search

Go to **Search → Visual Search → Enable**. Luminos AI begins computing image embeddings for your product catalog. This background process takes 15–60 minutes depending on catalog size and completes without affecting your storefront performance.

<Note>
Visual search requires product images in your catalog. Products without images are excluded from visual search results.
</Note>

## Use cases

- **Fashion discovery** — shoppers photograph an outfit they like and find similar items in your store
- **Home decor matching** — upload a room photo to find furniture and accessories that match the style
- **Part identification** — photograph a component to find the matching replacement in your catalog
- **Social media inspiration** — screenshot a product from Instagram and search your store for alternatives

## Configuration

Under **Search → Visual Search → Settings**, configure:

- **Result count** — number of visually similar products to return (default 12)
- **Similarity threshold** — minimum similarity score to include a result (0.0 to 1.0, default 0.5)
- **Category filtering** — optionally restrict visual search results to the same category as the uploaded image's detected category
- **Combine with text** — allow shoppers to upload an image and type a text query simultaneously (e.g. upload a red dress photo and type "under £50")

## Image requirements

For best results, uploaded images should:

- Be at least 200×200 pixels
- Contain a clearly visible product against a non-cluttered background
- Be in JPEG, PNG, or WebP format
- Be under 10 MB in file size

The widget validates these requirements and shows a helpful error message if the image does not meet them.

## Monitoring

Track visual search usage in [Search analytics](/search/search-analytics) under the **Visual search** tab. Key metrics include upload count, result CTR, and conversion rate compared to text search.
