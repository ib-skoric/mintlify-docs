Luminos AI Smart Search replaces your storefront's default search with an AI-powered engine that understands intent, tolerates typos, and learns from how shoppers interact with results. The goal is to connect every search query to the most relevant products as fast as possible.

Smart Search combines text matching, behavioural signals, and merchandising rules into a single ranking pipeline. Results improve automatically over time as the engine learns which products get clicked and purchased for each query.

## Key capabilities

- **Typo tolerance** — "runing shoes" still returns running shoes
- **Synonym expansion** — "sofa" matches products tagged as "couch"
- **Natural language understanding** — queries like "blue dress under 50 dollars" are parsed into filters
- **Personalised ranking** — returning visitors see results influenced by their browsing history
- **Merchandising controls** — pin, boost, or bury products for specific queries
